No external source acquisition is required
------------------------------------------
All bundled examples, conformance vectors, and validation fixtures are synthetic project-authored structures. The package does not require external audit datasets, financial records, policy documents, accounting statements, source PDFs, APIs, databases, or network access.

Reproduction boundary
---------------------
Reproducing a certificate or proof ledger establishes deterministic behavior under the declared structural contract. It does not verify the truth, provenance, legal sufficiency, or professional-audit adequacy of external evidence represented by a caller.

Project-supplied cross-language verification
--------------------------------------------
The JavaScript verifier is separately implemented and does not call the Python producer. This strengthens portability and implementation independence, but it remains project-supplied validation rather than third-party reproduction.

Independent reproduction
------------------------
Status: OPEN

A stronger third-party reproduction should independently implement or inspect the published portable format specification, regenerate or verify the synthetic vectors and proof lineage, test adversarial mutations independently, and report both successful and refused cases without weakening the claim boundaries.
