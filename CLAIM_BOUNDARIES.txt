SLANG-AUDIT — CLAIM BOUNDARIES v2.4.0

Permitted framing
-----------------
- bounded structural resolution of caller-declared audit evidence, rules, and controls;
- deterministic canonicalization and target-specific verdicts under the declared Boolean audit contract;
- proof-carrying certificates whose semantics are recomputed during verification;
- target dependency isolation and exact bounded minimal sufficient witnesses;
- exact bounded minimal verdict cuts and structural completion/repair counterfactuals;
- predecessor-bound incremental proof deltas with full-recomputation equivalence;
- tamper-evident declared audit lineage with deterministic checkpoints and branch comparison;
- evidence commitments as identity-binding declarations only.

Required qualifications
-----------------------
- all evidence claims are caller-declared structural inputs;
- evidence commitments bind declared identities but do not authenticate underlying source material;
- minimal witnesses and counterfactuals are exact only within declared resource bounds and the admitted structural model;
- incremental verification preserves target-local proofs only when the recomputed dependency analysis supports preservation;
- checkpoint verification establishes identity with a previously pinned structural lineage, not historical authenticity by itself;
- a self-consistent alternative history can form a different valid lineage unless compared with a previously pinned checkpoint;
- independent third-party reproduction remains open.

Not claimed
-----------
- external truth verification;
- external source provenance verification;
- replacement for professional auditing, assurance, attestation, accounting judgment, legal review, regulatory procedure, or internal-control testing;
- audit opinion authority, certification, accreditation, institutional endorsement, or compliance approval;
- authenticity of bank records, financial statements, access logs, policies, inventories, datasets, signatures, timestamps, identities, or evidence sources;
- that replay or reconciliation is unnecessary in real-world audits;
- that verification is unnecessary or non-fundamental;
- that structural PASS means a real-world audit opinion should be issued;
- universal correctness across undeclared semantics, laws, standards, industries, or evidence types;
- cryptographic signatures, trusted timestamping, decentralized consensus, or external publication of checkpoints;
- performance superiority over full recomputation.

OPTIONAL CONTENT-BINDING BOUNDARY
-------------------------------
The separate evidence content-binding verifier can establish exact supplied-byte equality with a declared SHA-256 commitment. It does not establish source provenance, authenticity, completeness, truth, legal validity, or trusted time.
